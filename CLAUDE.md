# AI Journey Community Website

Community website and blogs for AI Journey, the student AI club at AUB (American University of Beirut). Readers are university students, mostly CS and engineering, reading mainly on phones.

## Read before working
- docs/01-what-we-do.md: mission and what every issue contains
- docs/02-audience-icp.md: who reads it
- docs/03-voice-and-personality.md: voice and hard writing rules
- docs/04-example-output.md: a full sample issue (all invented, used as placeholder content)
- docs/inspiration/: screenshots of the original look we wanted (local only, not on GitHub)
- public/logo/: official logos
- PROMPT.md: the full build brief

## Hard rules
- Never use the em dash character (—) anywhere: UI text, content, alt text, meta tags, comments shown to users. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
- No cursing, ever.
- Mobile first. Every page must work at 360px with no horizontal scrolling, and look great at 1440px.
- Blue theme only. Cyan is the single accent. No red or orange.

## Brand tokens (sampled from the logo)
- navy #1C3C58 (logo outline), deep blue #004068 (logo wordmark), cyan #7CC8D4 (logo brain and waveform), light #E8ECF0 (light logo)
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans, 650 to 750 weight. Body and UI: Inter. Use strong, compact sans headings and comfortable reading text.
