# 02: Audience (Ideal Reader Profile)

*Who we write for. Every story, tool and game should pass the test: "Would this reader care, and would they understand it?" Update after reader feedback, polls or analytics.*

## Primary reader: the engineering / CS student

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Who** | Undergraduates in computer science, computer and electrical engineering, and other engineering majors, years 1 to 4 |
| **What they already know** | Use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for homework and code; know the basic idea of LLMs; depth varies a lot between year 1 and year 4 |
| **What they want** | Stay current without spending hours; tools that save time on coding, projects and studying; signals about which skills matter for jobs and internships; cool things to talk about |
| **Pain points** | Too much AI news and hype, hard to tell what is real; worry about AI and jobs; unsure what AI use is allowed in their courses; overwhelmed by tools |
| **Where and how they read** | On their phone, between classes or in the evening; arrive from Instagram or WhatsApp groups [TO CONFIRM] |
| **What makes them stop reading** | Walls of text, unexplained jargon, hype, things they already knew, anything that feels like an ad |

## Secondary reader: the curious non-tech student

Business, medicine, architecture, sciences, humanities and others.

- **Wants:** plain explanations, tools for their own field, and what AI means for their future career.
- **Needs from us:** no assumed technical background; every technical term explained in a few words the first time it appears.

## How we serve both in one issue

- Main text is readable by anyone; extra technical depth goes in the "Go deeper" link.
- Every story ends with a "Why it matters to you" or "So what" line.
- Tool of the Week includes a **Best for** line naming the majors it helps most.
- Over a month, Tool of the Week should rotate across majors (e.g. coding, writing, design, data, research).

## Quick personas

- **Karim, 3rd-year CS.** Follows some AI news already. Wants the signal, not the noise, and tools that actually help his projects and internship hunt.
- **Maya, 1st-year engineering.** Uses ChatGPT daily but doesn't know what a "model release" means. Wants to catch up without feeling lost.
- **Lina, 2nd-year business.** Curious but intimidated. Wants to know which AI tools help with reports and presentations, and whether her job is at risk.
