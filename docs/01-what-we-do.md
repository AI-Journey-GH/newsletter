# 01: What We Do

*The AI Journey newsletter: goal, scope and boundaries. Read this before writing or planning any issue. Update it whenever the goals change.*

## Mission in one line

Every week, help university students keep up with AI (what happened, why it matters to them, and how to use it in their own major) in one 10-15 minute read.

## Who publishes it

The **AI Journey** club. [TO CONFIRM: university, editor-in-chief, who writes which section, who proofreads]

## What every issue gives the reader

1. **News they can trust.** The week's most important AI news, focused on Silicon Valley and the big labs but not limited to them: what leaders are saying, new models, failures and incidents, discoveries and papers, regulation, and fun or weird stories.
2. **A practical skill.** One AI tool per week, with a short tutorial and a note on which majors it helps most.
3. **Something to learn.** A rotating Wildcard: a paper decoded, a concept explained, or an opportunities board.
4. **A reason to come back.** A weekly game or open question, answered in the next issue with shout-outs.

## Goals for the first semester

- **Habit.** Students open it every week, not just once.
- **Usefulness across majors.** It makes AI actionable for CS and engineering students and approachable for everyone else.
- **Community.** Readers play the game, send tips, and join AI Journey events.

## What we are NOT

- **Not a hype feed.** No story goes in without a credible source; company claims are labeled as claims.
- **Not a research journal.** We explain; we don't try to impress.
- **Not an ad.** Every tool gets an honest price and an honest limitation.
- **Not long for its own sake.** Quiet weeks are shorter; we never pad.
